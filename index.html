Introduction & Developer Identity

ChillHub (Android package identifier: com.chillhub.musicplayer, version 1.0) is an Android music discovery and streaming player developed by Saavej Choudhary under DNS Creations.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy describes exactly what data ChillHub processes locally on your Android device, what permissions it requests, and what requests are transmitted to external music catalog, artwork, and playback services based on the actual implementation of the application.

For any privacy questions, concerns, or requests, you may contact the developer directly at savejch7464@gmail.com.

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the ChillHub Android application (supporting Android 7.0 / API 24 and higher). It covers:

  • Data entered or generated inside ChillHub and stored locally on your device.
  • Device permissions declared by ChillHub and when Android asks for your consent.
  • Network requests sent directly from your device to third-party music, artwork, speech-recognition, or embedded video-playback providers.

This policy does not govern the independent privacy practices of external third-party platforms (such as JioSaavn endpoints, Google/YouTube services, or your device’s built-in speech recognition provider) when your device connects to them to fetch search results, artwork, or streams.

Information You Provide Directly

ChillHub does not require account registration, sign-in, email verification, phone numbers, or passwords. Any personalizing information you enter inside the app is stored locally on your device only:

  • Display Name / Profile Name: During onboarding or inside Profile & Settings, you may optionally enter or edit a display name (stored in the local app_settings database table). It is used solely to personalize greetings and “Made For You” labels on your screen and is not uploaded by ChillHub to any developer server.
  • Playlists & Favorites: Songs you mark as favorites and custom playlists you create (including playlist titles, track metadata, and artwork URLs) are saved locally in the app’s database.
  • Music Search Queries: Text or voice queries you enter are saved in your local search_history table for quick access and sent to the active music search endpoints solely to retrieve matching songs, albums, and artists.
  • App Preferences & Playback Settings: Audio quality choice, theme preference, active stream source preference, shuffle/repeat mode, playback volume, and onboarding completion flag are saved locally.
  • Support Emails (User-Initiated): If you tap the feedback/contact option in Profile & Settings or email savejch7464@gmail.com, your email app opens outside ChillHub and whatever details you choose to include in your email are processed according to standard email delivery.

Automatically Collected & Technical Information

To provide music playback, adaptive audio quality, and continuous listening, ChillHub processes limited technical and activity information locally on your device and sends standard network requests over HTTPS:

  • Local Listening Activity & Personalization Signals: ChillHub stores your recently_played tracks, play counts, timestamps, saved playback_queue position, and local listening_preferences (artist/category/language weights derived from tracks you play or favorite) inside its on-device Room database to resume playback and personalize your home feed locally.
  • Network Connectivity & Bandwidth State: Using Android’s ConnectivityManager, ChillHub checks whether your device is online, whether the network is metered (cellular) or unmetered (Wi-Fi), and estimated downstream bandwidth so automatic audio quality and offline notices work properly.
  • Standard Network Request Metadata: Whenever your device fetches music metadata, artwork, or streams from third-party APIs or CDNs, those external servers automatically receive standard technical HTTP metadata (such as your IP address, user-agent/device networking headers, and requested song/album/video IDs or search keywords).
  • Image & WebView Caching: Album/artist thumbnails downloaded via Coil are cached locally in your app cache directory (up to 50 MB). When playing YouTube tracks through the embedded YouTube IFrame Player, Android’s WebView may maintain standard browser/DOM storage required for playback.
What ChillHub Does NOT Collect: ChillHub does not read your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, photos/videos, local audio files on shared storage, precise GPS location, or Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).

Android Permissions & Runtime Consent

Android distinguishes between install-time (normal/service) permissions granted automatically when you install the app, and runtime permissions that trigger a system permission popup asking for your explicit approval before a sensitive feature runs.

Android Permission Consent Type & Popup Why ChillHub Needs It If Denied or Restricted
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Runtime Popup Triggered only when you tap the microphone icon on the Search screen to perform voice search using Android’s speech recognizer. Voice search does not run; an inline notice appears and standard typed music search continues to work normally.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Popup (Android 13+) Requested once per session when direct audio playback starts so ChillHub can display media playback and lock-screen controls. Notification controls are hidden, while in-app and background audio playback continue without interruption.
android.permission.INTERNET Install-Time (No Popup) Required to search music catalogs, load album artwork, fetch streams, and load the embedded YouTube player. Streaming and online search require an active internet connection.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-Time (No Popup) Used to detect offline status and metered/bandwidth conditions for automatic audio quality selection. Not revocable by user; helps prevent failed requests offline.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Install-Time (No Popup) Allows ChillHubPlaybackService (Media3 MediaSessionService) to continue playing audio when you switch apps or lock your screen (Android 14+ media playback service type). System-managed permission; required by Android OS for background audio playback.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Install-Time (No Popup) Used by Media3 ExoPlayer during network streaming and by AlarmManager to trigger your optional Sleep Timer accurately. System-managed permission; prevents playback stalls during streaming.
com.chillhub.musicplayer.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION Auto-Added by AndroidX (No Popup) Added automatically at build time by AndroidX/Jetpack libraries to isolate internal app broadcast receivers from external apps. Internal OS security signature permission; not user-facing.

Permissions Explicitly NOT Used: ChillHub does not declare or request READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, CAMERA, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, or com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID.

Voice Search & Microphone Processing

ChillHub provides an optional microphone button in the Search screen for hands-free voice search:

  • ChillHub never accesses your microphone in the background or without you tapping the voice-search icon.
  • When tapped, ChillHub checks for RECORD_AUDIO permission and displays Android’s system permission dialog if not yet granted.
  • When active, ChillHub uses Android’s built-in SpeechRecognizer (RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH) to pass spoken audio to your device’s active OS speech recognition service (such as Google Speech Services or your device default provider).
  • No Audio Recording Storage by ChillHub: ChillHub does not record, save, or upload raw audio files to any server controlled by DNS Creations; it only receives the converted text transcription to populate your music search query.
  • You can revoke microphone permission anytime in Android System Settings and continue using text search normally.
7

Music Streaming, Playback & Background Notifications

Background audio playback is powered by Jetpack Media3 (ExoPlayer and MediaSessionService) via ChillHubPlaybackService:

  • Media Controls Notification: On Android 13 (API 33) and above, ChillHub requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS once per session when direct playback starts so Media3 can display track title, artist, artwork, and play/pause/skip controls in the notification shade and lock screen.
  • Playback Continuity on Denial: If you decline notification permission, ChillHub still plays your music cleanly without displaying notification controls.
  • Sleep Timer: If you enable the in-app Sleep Timer, ChillHub schedules a local wake alarm via Android’s AlarmManager to pause playback automatically when the timer expires.

Third-Party APIs, Embedded Players & External Services

To provide music search, album tracklists, artist pages, artwork, and streaming, ChillHub communicates directly from your device with external third-party services. ChillHub does not operate a proprietary backend server that collects user profiles.

  • JioSaavn Catalog Endpoints & Community API Proxy: ChillHub queries JioSaavn web API endpoints (www.jiosaavn.com), an unofficial community-hosted JioSaavn-compatible endpoint (jiosavan-coderaryan.vercel.app), and JioSaavn media CDNs (saavncdn.com) to retrieve song, album, artist, and stream URL metadata. When queried, those servers receive your search terms, selected item IDs/URLs, and standard HTTP connection metadata (such as IP address).
  • YouTube Data API v3 (Optional Configuration): When a YouTube API key is configured in the app build, ChillHub queries www.googleapis.com/youtube/v3 with your search query, region code (IN), language preference, or video ID to locate music videos/tracks.
  • Embedded YouTube IFrame Player (WebView): When playing a YouTube-sourced track, ChillHub loads https://www.youtube.com/iframe_api inside an Android WebView with JavaScript and DOM storage enabled (domStorageEnabled = true). Inside that embedded player, YouTube/Google independently processes video requests, IP address, device/WebView metadata, and browser/DOM storage or cookies in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy and YouTube’s Terms of Service.
  • Coil Image Loader & OkHttp / Retrofit: Open-source Android networking and image caching libraries running strictly inside the app to fetch and cache album/artist artwork and API responses.
  • Lyrics Status: In the current version, ChillHub’s lyrics provider is disabled locally and does not send track data to any external lyrics API.
  • Device Speech Recognition Service: Receives audio only when you explicitly tap Voice Search.

Please note that external third-party APIs and embedded media players operate under their own independent privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing Google/YouTube’s privacy disclosures and applicable provider policies for details on how they handle connection logs and cookies.

Local Storage, Caching & Device Backups

ChillHub stores user data locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms:

  • Local Room SQLite Database (chillhub_music.db): Stores your favorites, custom playlists, playlist songs, recently played history, search history, local listening preferences, active playback queue, and app settings (including your optional display name).
  • Artwork Disk Cache: Coil stores up to 50 MB of album and artist thumbnail images in the app’s private cache folder (chillhub_image_cache).
  • WebView Storage: Android’s WebView cache and DOM storage are used by the embedded YouTube IFrame player during YouTube playback.
  • Android Auto Backup: ChillHub’s Android manifest includes android:allowBackup="true" with standard Android backup configuration files. Depending on your Android OS version and personal Google device backup settings, the Android operating system may back up app-private database data to your personal device backup account unless excluded or disabled in your device settings.

Advertising, Analytics & Tracking Disclosure

Based on ChillHub’s verified codebase and build dependencies:

  • No Advertising SDKs: ChillHub displays no third-party ads and does not include AdMob or any ad mediation SDK.

Data Security & Limitations

ChillHub applies practical technical safeguards appropriate to a local-first music player:

  • Encrypted Transport (HTTPS): Catalog, artwork, YouTube API, and embedded player requests use HTTPS endpoints.
  • Private App Sandbox: Your Room database and image cache reside inside Android’s private app storage directory, protected by OS sandboxing from other non-privileged apps.
  • Release Build Logging Disabled: Network HTTP logging (HttpLoggingInterceptor) is strictly disabled (Level.NONE) in release builds, and sensitive headers/API keys are redacted in debug logs.

Important Limitation: ChillHub’s local Room database is not additionally encrypted at rest by a separate app-level encryption layer beyond standard Android device encryption, and no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Data Retention & Your Controls

Because ChillHub stores your library and preferences locally on your device rather than on developer-hosted servers, you stay in full control of your data:

  • Edit or Clear Inside ChillHub: You can update your display name in Profile & Settings, delete individual search queries or clear all search history, remove favorites, delete playlists, and toggle app settings anytime.
  • Clear All Local Data: You can erase the entire local database, image cache, and WebView storage at any time via Android Settings → Apps → ChillHub → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling ChillHub.
  • Permission Controls: You can grant or revoke Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) and Notification (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) permissions at any time in Android App Settings.

ChillHub does not control server-side access log retention of independent third-party endpoints (such as JioSaavn endpoints, the community API proxy, or Google/YouTube services).
